Privacy Policy for Drakonir: Apex Raid

Effective date: 4 October 2026
Last updated: 4 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Thanapat Wongchalermthan (“we”, “us”, or “the developer”) handles information in the Android game Drakonir: Apex Raid (package name com.drakonir.apexraid).

Drakonir: Apex Raid is a single-player action game. You do not create an account. We do not operate servers that store your gameplay. The data described below is either kept on your device or collected by Google when an ad is shown.

1. Who this policy covers

This policy applies to anyone who downloads or plays Drakonir: Apex Raid from Google Play or another authorized source. It does not cover websites, ads, or services that you open from an advertisement, which have their own policies.

2. Information stored on your device

The game saves your progress in one file in the app’s private storage so you can continue later. It contains:

  • Gold, fragments, rune chests and other upgrade currencies
  • Which Hunters you have recruited, their ranks, and your runes and keystones
  • Achievements, tutorial progress and play statistics (for example runs, clears and best time)
  • Your settings (volume, camera effects, controls)
  • How many rewarded ads you have watched today and how many ad rewards are waiting to be used (see section 4)

This information stays on your device. We cannot read it remotely. We do not operate a cloud save. If Android Backup is active on your device, the system may include this local save in a device backup.

The game does not ask for access to your photos, contacts, microphone, camera, precise location, or files. All art and audio are built into the app.

3. Information we do not collect ourselves

We do not collect or store:

  • Your name, email address, or phone number (unless you email us)
  • Payment information (the game has no purchases; ads are the only monetization)
  • A login or player profile
  • Precise GPS location
  • Photos or files from your device

If you email us for support, we will use that message only to reply and will not add you to a marketing list.

4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free-to-play version of the game offers optional rewarded ads from Google AdMob. When you tap to watch an ad, finishing it earns one Hunter recruitment. You can earn at most five per day.

There are no banner ads and no automatic interstitial ads. A rewarded ad plays only if you tap to watch it, and the game is fully playable without watching any.

Google’s advertising SDK may collect and process information such as:

  • Android Advertising ID
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it (not GPS)
  • Device type, operating system, language, and similar technical data
  • Ad views, clicks, and whether a rewarded ad was completed
  • Diagnostic information about ad loading and display

Google uses this information to serve and measure ads, limit how often you see the same ad, detect fraud and invalid traffic, and (where you allow it) personalize ads. We do not receive a name or email from this process. We do not sell your information.

Google’s own disclosures:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from ads
  • AdMob and your data

5. Consent choices

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the game shows Google’s consent form (Google User Messaging Platform) on first launch. The game requests ads only after consent has been given or is not required. Where the law requires it, the Settings page has an “Ad privacy options” button that reopens the form so you can change your choice at any time.

6. Permissions

The app requests these Android permissions:

  • Internet — to load ads
  • Network state — to know whether a connection is available before requesting an ad
  • Advertising ID — used by Google Mobile Ads / AdMob as described above

7. How we use information

On-device game data is used only to:

  • Save and restore your progress, Hunters, runes and settings
  • Apply the daily limit of five rewarded ads and pay out the reward

Advertising data is used by Google to show, measure, and (where permitted) personalize ads, and to keep the ad system secure.

8. Legal bases (EEA, UK, and Switzerland)

  • Saving your progress on your device is based on our legitimate interest in providing the game you chose to play, and on performance of the service you requested.
  • Personalized advertising, and storing or reading information on your device for advertising, is based on your consent given in Google’s consent form.
  • Fraud prevention and ad measurement that the ad platform requires to function may rely on legitimate interests as described by Google.

9. Sharing

We do not sell personal information. We do not share your on-device game progress with anyone.

Ads are provided by Google. Google may share advertising data with its own partners as described in Google’s policies. We do not control those partners.

We may disclose information if required by law, or to defend legal claims, or if you ask us to in writing.

10. Your choices

  • Change your consent. Use “Ad privacy options” on the Settings page where it is shown (section 5).
  • Reset or delete Advertising ID. On Android, open Settings, then Privacy (or Google), then Ads. You can reset the Advertising ID or opt out of ads personalization.
  • Skip rewarded ads. They are optional.
  • Clear game data. Uninstalling the game deletes your progress from that device. Android Settings → Apps → Drakonir: Apex Raid → Storage → Clear data does the same while leaving the app installed.

11. Retention

Game progress remains on the device until you clear app data or uninstall. We have no gameplay copy to delete on a server.

Google retains advertising data according to Google’s own retention periods. Support emails are kept only as long as needed to handle your request.

12. Security

Game data is stored in the app’s private storage on your device. The app uses encrypted connections (HTTPS) for ad traffic. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure. Keep your device locked and updated.

13. Children

Drakonir: Apex Raid contains fantasy combat and advertising. It is not directed at children under 13 (or under 16 where that is the digital age of consent). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. The Play target audience is not children.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe a child has provided personal information through an ad or by emailing us, contact us and we will delete what we hold. Because ads are shown, parents should use Google Play family controls if they do not want a child to use this app.

14. International processing

We are based in Thailand. Google may process advertising data in the United States and other countries. Those countries may have different data-protection laws from your own. Google describes its transfer safeguards in its privacy policy.

15. California and similar US state laws

We do not sell personal information. Sharing of Advertising ID and related device data with Google for advertising may be considered “sharing” for cross-context behavioral advertising under some US state laws. You can opt out of ads personalization in Android Settings as described in section 10.

We do not use sensitive personal information to infer characteristics about you. We do not have a server-side profile of players that we can look up by name.

16. Your rights (EEA, UK, and similar laws)

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict, or object to processing of personal data, and to data portability. You may also lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

For data that exists only on your device, use Clear data or uninstall (section 10). For advertising data held by Google, use Google’s tools and policies linked in section 4, or email us and we will point you to the right Google request form. We cannot delete Google’s ad records on your behalf beyond what Google allows publishers to do.

17. Changes

We may update this policy if the game, ads, or the law changes. The “Last updated” date at the top will change. Continued use after an update means you accept the revised policy. Material changes will be reflected in the Play Store listing when required.
